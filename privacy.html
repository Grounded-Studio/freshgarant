Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

FreshGarant is built around one principle: your inventory is your business, not ours. Most of the app runs on your device. This page explains exactly what does leave your phone, why, and what control you have. Everything that leaves your phone is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

1. Who we are

FreshGarant is published by Grounded Studio, based in the Netherlands. Contact: contact@grounded-studio.app. For privacy requests, email the same address with "Privacy request" in the subject.

2. What stays on your device

Your product list, categories, storage locations, expiry dates, prices, notes, consumption history, waste log, shopping list, achievements, settings, saved supermarkets, home location and the product photos you take are stored only on your phone. Your precise GPS location never leaves your phone.

3. What leaves your device, and why

3.1 Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

We log a small set of events to understand which parts of the app are used — for example which screens are opened, which add method is used, and whether a purchase succeeded. We send no names, no product names, no photos and no receipt text; quantities are bucketed into ranges (e.g. "5–15 items"). Firebase also records technical data automatically: an app-instance ID, device model, Android version and approximate country. We do not collect your advertising ID.

Processor: Google (Firebase). Our Google Analytics data-sharing settings are switched off, so Google does not use this data for its own purposes. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the app. You can switch this off at any time: Settings → Data → Share usage statistics.

3.2 AI features (Google Gemini) — only when you use them

  • AI Fridge Scan: the photo you take of your fridge.
  • AI Recipe and AI Weekly Meal Plan: the names of products in your inventory.
  • Searching a supermarket by name: the text you type, so Gemini can recognise the store and city.

We use the paid Gemini API. Under Google's terms, this data is not used to train Google's models; Google may keep it for a limited period to detect abuse. We do not store it ourselves.

Receipt scan and barcode scanning use Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

3.3 Family sharing (optional, PRO)

If you create or join a family, the app signs you in with an anonymous Firebase ID (no email or password). Shared through Google Cloud Firestore: the products you add (name, brand, barcode, dates, quantity, price, notes), the shared shopping list, a short activity log ("Anna added milk") and the display name you choose. Product photos you take stay on your phone.

When you leave a family, your membership is removed; products you added stay visible to the remaining members. When the last member leaves, the family and all its data are deleted.

3.4 Invite friends (optional)

To give you and a friend a free PRO month, we store your invite code together with your anonymous Firebase ID and the anonymous IDs of people who used it. No names or email addresses.

3.5 Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode we look it up in the free Open Food Facts database for product name, brand and nutrition info. Only the barcode number is sent.

3.6 Supermarkets and location (optional)

  • Nearby-store alerts: while the app is open, your location is compared on your phone with your saved supermarkets. It is not sent anywhere and never used in the background.
  • Finding supermarkets near you: we send an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, to OpenStreetMap services in the EU (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee and nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Searching by name sends only the text you type.

3.7 Google Play (purchases, updates, ratings)

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only whether your subscription is active, and our statistics record which plan was bought (monthly or yearly) — never card or billing details. In-app updates and ratings also run through Google Play.

3.8 This website

freshgarant.app sets no cookies and loads no analytics or tracking scripts.

Contact form: your name, email address, topic and message are sent through our own server (hosted by STRATO in Germany) to our inbox. We use them only to reply and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. You can also email us directly.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No account, email or password is needed to use the app.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS or microphone.
  • No advertising ID and no advertising SDKs. There are no ads in FreshGarant.
  • No precise location leaves your phone.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

  • Copy of your data: export your products via Settings → Data → Export CSV. Family data is visible on the family screen.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Data → Delete all my data. This clears the app's database and settings on your phone, removes you from your family (deleting the family if you are the last member), deletes your invite code and deletes your anonymous Firebase ID. No longer have the app? See delete your data.
  • Stop usage statistics: Settings → Data → Share usage statistics.
  • Location: withdraw the permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Complaints: you can contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

6. Data retention

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Usage statistics: 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Family data: until the last member leaves or you delete your data.
  • Invite codes: until you delete your data.
  • AI requests: not stored by us; Google per its Gemini API terms.
  • Contact messages: until no longer needed to help you.

7. Children

FreshGarant is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and want their data removed, email us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we update the "last updated" date and, where appropriate, show a notice in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: contact@grounded-studio.app.
