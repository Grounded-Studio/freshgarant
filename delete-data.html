YOUR DATA

Delete your FreshGarant data

FreshGarant has no user accounts — no email address or password. Most of your data lives only on your phone. If you use family sharing or invite friends, the app creates an anonymous Firebase ID to link that data to your phone.

Option 1 — in the app (immediate)

Open FreshGarant → Settings → Data → Delete all my data. This deletes:

  • On your phone: your inventory, waste log, shopping list, product photos and settings.
  • In the cloud: your family membership (and the whole family if you are the last member), your invite code and your anonymous Firebase ID.

Option 2 — by email (if the app is no longer installed)

Email contact@grounded-studio.app with the subject "Delete my data" and include your family code and/or invite code if you know them. Because your data is not linked to your name or email address, these codes are how we find it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Good to know

  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone, but not family or invite data in the cloud. Use option 1 before uninstalling.
  • Usage statistics are not linked to your name and are deleted automatically after 14 months. You can switch them off in Settings → Data → Share usage statistics.
  • Purchases are managed by Google Play. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
