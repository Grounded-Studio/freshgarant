FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is "What's for dinner?"

Take one photo of your fridge, or use the products already in the app, and FreshGarant suggests three dinners you can make tonight with what you have, using what expires first. Products on the photo can be added to your stock in one tap. Free users get one idea per day, PRO up to ten.

What is FreshGarant?

FreshGarant is a free food expiry tracker for Android. It keeps track of the expiry dates of everything in your fridge, freezer and pantry, reminds you before food goes bad, and suggests recipes that use what you already have.

Is FreshGarant free?

Yes. The free version tracks up to 40 products and includes barcode scanning, daily expiry reminders, the smart shopping list and savings tracking. PRO costs €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year and adds unlimited products and all AI features. The monthly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Every free user can try each AI feature once.

How does FreshGarant know when my food expires?

Scan a barcode and FreshGarant fills in the product details and an expiry date that you can adjust. You can also pick a date yourself with one tap, or let the AI fridge scan suggest dates for everything in a single photo.

Do I need an account?

No. Your inventory is stored on your own phone. The app only goes online for things like barcode lookups, AI features, finding supermarkets and anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is sent.

Is FreshGarant available for iPhone?

Not yet. FreshGarant is currently available for Android on Google Play. An iOS version is on the roadmap.

How much money can I save?

According to WRAP, a UK household of four throws away about £1,000 of edible food a year. A lot of it is food that expired unnoticed, which is what FreshGarant helps prevent. Use the savings calculator to estimate your own savings, and the app shows what you actually saved.
