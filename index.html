WHAT IT DOES

Three ways FreshGarant actually helps.

Not another to-do list for food. A real workflow built around the moment food goes bad.

📦

Scan, don't type

Scan a barcode, a receipt or your whole fridge with AI. Products land in your inventory with name, category, storage and expiry date auto-filled.

🔔

Reminders before it's too late

A single daily morning digest tells you what expires today, tomorrow and this week. Freeze-it hints for items you can save.

🍳

Cook what's fresh

Tap the Cook tab — recipes are matched to your real fridge, prioritising items that expire soonest. Gemini AI generates one on the fly if nothing fits.
