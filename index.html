FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is FreshGarant?

FreshGarant is a free food expiry tracker for Android. It keeps track of the expiry dates of everything in your fridge, freezer and pantry, reminds you before food goes bad, and suggests recipes that use what you already have.

Is FreshGarant free?

Yes. The free version tracks up to 40 products and includes barcode scanning, daily expiry reminders, the smart shopping list and savings tracking. PRO costs €2.99 per month or €19.99 per year and adds unlimited products and all AI features. Every free user gets one free trial of each AI feature.

How does FreshGarant know when my food expires?

Scan a barcode and FreshGarant fills in the product details and an expiry date that you can adjust. You can also pick a date yourself with one tap, or let the AI fridge scan suggest dates for everything in a single photo.

Do I need an account?

No. Your inventory is stored on your own phone. The app only goes online for things like barcode lookups, AI features, finding supermarkets and anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is sent.

Is FreshGarant available for iPhone?

Not yet. FreshGarant is currently available for Android on Google Play. An iOS version is on the roadmap.

How much money can I save?

An average household throws away around €540 of food every year. Most of it is food that simply expired unnoticed, which is exactly what FreshGarant prevents. Use the savings calculator to estimate your own savings.
