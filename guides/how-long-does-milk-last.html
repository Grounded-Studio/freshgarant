STORAGE GUIDE

How long does milk last after opening?

Short answer: Once opened, milk keeps about 1 week in the fridge at 4°C, according to the Voedingscentrum. An unopened carton follows the date on the pack. Milk can be frozen for about 2 months. Check the label first: milk with a best before date can still be fine after it, milk with a use by date cannot.

Best before or use by? Check your carton

Milk can carry either date, depending on how it is made. That matters, because the two dates mean very different things:

  • Best before is about quality. The FSA says food with a best-before date can be eaten after the date, and you can use your senses to check it. The Voedingscentrum likewise says not to throw milk away straight after the best-before date: look, smell and taste a little.
  • Use by is about safety. The FSA warns that a sniff test is not safe for any food with a use-by date, because you cannot smell the bacteria that cause food poisoning.

In the Netherlands most fresh milk carries a best-before date (THT). In the UK, both are common, so always look at which one your carton has.

How long does opened milk last?

The Voedingscentrum gives about 1 week in the fridge for opened semi-skimmed milk. Many cartons also say "once opened, use within x days": follow that instruction when it is on the pack. Long-life (UHT) milk can be kept in the cupboard until opened; after opening it belongs in the fridge as well.

How to store milk

  • Back in the fridge straight away. The Voedingscentrum advises putting milk back quickly after use, at 4°C. That keeps it good for longer.
  • Not in the door if you can avoid it. The door is the warmest part of the fridge and warms up every time you open it.
  • Close the carton. Milk takes on smells from other food.

Can you freeze milk?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum gives about 2 months in the freezer. Leave some room in the bottle or carton, because milk expands when it freezes. Defrost it in the fridge and shake well: the fat can separate, which is harmless. Defrosted milk is best for cooking, baking or smoothies.

How to tell if milk has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: milk that smells or tastes sour, or has lumps. If you see or smell any of these, pour it away. For milk with a best-before date, this check is exactly how you decide whether it is still good after the date. For milk with a use-by date, the date decides, not your nose.

Tips to waste less milk

  • Buy the size you actually use in a week.
  • Milk that is nearly finished can go into pancakes, porridge, a white sauce or a smoothie.
  • Freeze part of a large bottle if you will not finish it in time.
  • Log it in FreshGarant with the date you opened it, so you know when to finish it.

Frequently asked questions

Can you drink milk after the best-before date?

Often yes. Best before is about quality. Look, smell and taste a little: if it is not sour or lumpy, it is fine.

Can you drink milk after the use-by date?

No. A use-by date is about safety, and you cannot smell the bacteria that cause food poisoning.

How long does opened milk last?

About 1 week in the fridge at 4°C, according to the Voedingscentrum, unless the carton says otherwise.

Can you freeze milk?

Yes, for about 2 months. Defrost it in the fridge and shake well before using.

Why does my milk go off before the date?

The date only applies to an unopened pack stored cold. Once opened, or if it is left out or kept in a warm fridge door, it spoils sooner.
