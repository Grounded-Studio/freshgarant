STORAGE GUIDE

How long do strawberries last?

Short answer: Strawberries keep 1 to 3 days in the fridge and only about 1 day in a fruit bowl, according to the Voedingscentrum. Leave the green tops on, keep them dry, and wash them just before eating. In the freezer they keep 8 to 12 months. Throw away mouldy strawberries and check the ones next to them.

Strawberries are delicate

Strawberries are among the most perishable fruits. The Voedingscentrum's storage guide gives just 1 to 3 days in the fridge, and about 1 day in a fruit bowl at room temperature. So if you will not eat them today, put them in the fridge.

How to keep strawberries fresh for longer

  • Leave the green tops on. According to the Voedingscentrum, strawberries keep longer with their crowns still attached.
  • In a closed container in the vegetable drawer. That is the Voedingscentrum's preferred spot.
  • Keep them dry. Moisture speeds up mould. Put them on kitchen paper to absorb moisture.
  • Wash just before eating, not before storing. The FSA advises washing fruit under running water or in a bowl of fresh water before you eat it.

Mould on strawberries

A soft or bruised strawberry is not immediately spoiled, says the Voedingscentrum. But throw away strawberries that are mouldy or clearly rotten, and check the strawberries that touched them extra carefully, because mould spreads quickly in a punnet. The FSA advises not eating fruit that contains mould, because cutting away the mouldy part does not guarantee that unseen toxins are gone.

Can you freeze strawberries?

Yes, and they keep a long time: 8 to 12 months, according to the Voedingscentrum. Cut off the tops first and cut the strawberries into pieces if you like, then freeze them in a sealed container or freezer bag. Freezing them on a tray first and then bagging them stops them from sticking together. Defrosted strawberries turn soft, so use them in smoothies, sauces, jam, yoghurt or baking rather than whole on a cake.

Overripe strawberries

The FSA notes that slightly mushy strawberries and other overripe fruit can be eaten normally, as long as there is no mould. Overripe fruit is great for cooking, baking and smoothies.

For the best flavour

Cold makes strawberries taste a little less sweet. Take them out of the fridge a short while before eating, but do not leave them at room temperature for long: in a fruit bowl, the Voedingscentrum gives them about a day. Only wash and hull the strawberries you are going to eat right away; the rest keep better with their tops on.

Freezing step by step

  1. Wash the strawberries and pat them completely dry.
  2. Cut off the green tops, and halve large strawberries.
  3. Spread them on a tray and freeze them for a few hours.
  4. Transfer them to a freezer bag or container, write the date on it, and keep them for 8 to 12 months.

Tips to waste fewer strawberries

  • Sort through the punnet when you get home and eat the softest ones first.
  • Freeze what you will not finish within two days.
  • Log strawberries in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder before they go soft.

Frequently asked questions

How long do strawberries last in the fridge?

1 to 3 days, according to the Voedingscentrum, in a closed container in the vegetable drawer.

Should you wash strawberries before storing them?

No, wash them just before eating. Moisture makes them go mouldy faster.

Can you eat strawberries if one is mouldy?

Throw away the mouldy ones and check the strawberries that touched them carefully. The rest can be eaten if they look fine.

Can you freeze strawberries?

Yes, for 8 to 12 months. Remove the tops first.

Are soft strawberries safe?

Yes, slightly mushy strawberries are fine as long as there is no mould. Use them in smoothies or sauces.

Do strawberries last longer with the tops on?

Yes. According to the Voedingscentrum, strawberries keep longer with their green crowns still attached.
