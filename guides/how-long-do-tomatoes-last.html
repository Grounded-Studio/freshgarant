STORAGE GUIDE

How long do tomatoes last?

Short answer: Tomatoes keep about 1 week in a fruit bowl, according to the Voedingscentrum. Keep them outside the fridge, because they lose their flavour in the cold. Store a cut tomato covered, cut side down. Blanched tomatoes can be frozen for about 3 months, for sauces and soups.

Keep tomatoes out of the fridge

The Voedingscentrum advises storing tomatoes outside the fridge, because they lose their flavour in the cold. In a fruit bowl or on the counter, out of direct sunlight, they keep about a week. Only very ripe tomatoes you cannot eat in time are better off in the fridge for a day; let them come back to room temperature before eating for the best taste.

How to keep a cut tomato

The Voedingscentrum's tip: put a cut tomato with the cut side down on a saucer, in a sealed container, or wrap it in (reusable) cling film, so it does not dry out. Use it within a day or two.

Soft or spoiled?

A soft tomato can be overripe but still usable, for example in a sauce, says the Voedingscentrum. But throw a tomato away if it is mouldy, slimy, leaking, rotten or smells off. The FSA advises not eating food that clearly contains mould, because cutting the mould away does not guarantee that unseen toxins are gone.

Do not eat unripe green tomatoes

Only eat tomatoes when they are ripe. The Voedingscentrum warns that unripe, green tomatoes contain the toxin tomatine, which can be harmful. This applies to unripe tomatoes, not to varieties that are naturally green when ripe. Let unripe tomatoes ripen on the counter first.

Can you freeze tomatoes?

Raw tomatoes are less suitable for freezing, says the Voedingscentrum: after freezing they are only good for sauces or soup. Blanched, they keep about 3 months in the freezer. Score a cross in the skin, dip them in boiling water for a moment and then in cold water, peel them and freeze them whole or chopped. Even easier: cook a batch of tomato sauce and freeze that in portions.

Tins and passata

Tinned tomatoes and passata keep a long time unopened. Once opened, the FSA advises not storing food in the opened tin: tip the contents into a storage container, cover it and put it in the fridge.

Ripening tomatoes

Tomatoes that are not fully ripe yet ripen best at room temperature, stalk side down, out of direct sunlight. According to the Voedingscentrum, a lot of fruit ripens faster next to bananas, so placing tomatoes near a bunch of bananas or in a paper bag with one speeds things up. Once ripe, eat them within a few days for the best flavour.

Tips to waste fewer tomatoes

  • Buy tomatoes at different stages of ripeness.
  • Turn soft tomatoes into sauce, soup or shakshuka.
  • Log tomatoes in FreshGarant, so the last ones do not get forgotten in the fruit bowl.

Frequently asked questions

How long do tomatoes last?

About a week in the fruit bowl or on the counter, according to the Voedingscentrum.

Should tomatoes go in the fridge?

No, they lose their flavour in the cold. Keep them outside the fridge.

Can you eat soft tomatoes?

Yes, overripe tomatoes are great in sauces. Throw them away if they are mouldy, slimy, leaking or smell off.

Can you freeze tomatoes?

Yes, blanched, for about 3 months. Use them in sauces or soups afterwards.

Are green tomatoes safe to eat?

Unripe green tomatoes contain tomatine, which can be harmful. Only eat ripe tomatoes.

How do you ripen tomatoes faster?

Keep them at room temperature next to bananas, or in a paper bag with a banana.
