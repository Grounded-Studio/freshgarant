How long does bread last?

Short answer: Bread keeps 2 to 4 days at room temperature, according to the Voedingscentrum: about 2–4 days for white bread and 4 days for wholemeal. In the freezer it keeps 1 to 3 months. How long does bread stay fresh?

The Voedingscentrum's storage guide gives 2 to 4 days at room temperature for white bread and about 4 days for wholemeal bread. Bread rarely becomes unsafe within that time; it mostly goes stale and dry. The real risk comes later, when mould appears.

Freeze it: fresh bread every day

The Voedingscentrum's main tip is simple: freeze bread in portions straight after buying, and take out what you need each day. Slices defrost in a few minutes on the counter or go straight into the toaster. This way you never have to throw away the end of a loaf.

  • White bread: about 1 month in the freezer.
  • Wholemeal bread: 1 to 3 months.
  • Mind the dryness: bread is safe in the freezer, but it does dry out. The longer it is frozen, the drier it gets, so wrap it well.

How to store bread at room temperature

  • Closed, in a bread bin or bag, so it does not dry out.
  • Cool and dry, away from the oven and direct sunlight. Warmth and moisture help mould grow.
  • Cut side down on a board, if you keep a loaf unwrapped for a short time.

Mould on bread: throw it away

It is tempting to tear off the mouldy corner, but mould on bread can spread through the soft crumb further than you can see. The FSA advises not eating food that clearly contains mould, because removing the mouldy part does not guarantee that unseen toxins are gone. That applies to the whole loaf or bag, not just the slice with the spot. This advice matters most for children, pregnant people, people over 65 and anyone with a weakened immune system.

How to tell if bread has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: bread that tastes or smells musty, is dry, or has mould. Dry bread is not spoiled; it is just stale. Musty-smelling or mouldy bread is.

What to do with stale bread

Stale bread is perfect for cooking. The Voedingscentrum suggests toasting it or making a toasted sandwich. Other ideas: croutons for soup or salad, breadcrumbs (blitz and freeze them), French toast, bread pudding or a summer salad with tomatoes.

Tips to waste less bread

  • Freeze half the loaf on the day you buy it.
  • Buy smaller loaves if you live alone.
  • Log your bread in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder to use or freeze it in time.

Frequently asked questions

How long does bread last at room temperature?

2 to 4 days for white bread and about 4 days for wholemeal, according to the Voedingscentrum.

Can you freeze bread?

Yes: white bread about 1 month, wholemeal 1 to 3 months. Freeze it in slices so you can take out what you need.

Can you cut mould off bread?

No. Mould can spread through the crumb further than you can see. Throw the loaf away.

Is stale bread safe to eat?

Yes. Dry or stale bread is not spoiled. Toast it, or use it for croutons or breadcrumbs.

Should you keep bread in the fridge?

The Voedingscentrum lists room temperature and the freezer for bread. To keep it longer, freeze it.
