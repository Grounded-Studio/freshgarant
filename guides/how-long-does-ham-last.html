STORAGE GUIDE

How long does sliced ham last after opening?

Short answer: Opened sliced ham and most cold cuts keep 3 to 5 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, and never past the use-by date. For older people, pregnant people, very young children and anyone with a weakened immune system, the advice is 2 to 3 days at most, because of listeria. Cold cuts can be frozen for about 3 months.

Why cold cuts need care: listeria

Sliced ham, chicken slices, roast beef and other cold cuts are eaten without cooking, so any bacteria on them are not killed. The Voedingscentrum points out that cold cuts are sensitive to the growth of harmful bacteria, which cannot be seen, smelled or tasted. The best known is listeria, which, unlike most bacteria, can still grow slowly in the fridge. The FSA also mentions listeria as an example of bacteria that cold temperatures do not stop completely.

How long do opened cold cuts last?

The Voedingscentrum's storage guide gives 3 to 5 days in the fridge for opened ham and most cold cuts, such as chicken slices and sliced meatloaf. Dry-cured sausage like salami keeps a little longer: 5 to 7 days once opened. In all cases, do not eat them after the use-by date.

Extra careful for vulnerable groups. For older people, pregnant people, very young children and people with a weakened immune system, the Voedingscentrum advises keeping opened cold cuts for no longer than 2 to 3 days, because of listeria.

How to store cold cuts

  • In the coldest spot of the fridge, at 4°C, which is often the bottom shelf, the Voedingscentrum advises.
  • In a sealed container or the original packaging, so they do not dry out.
  • Use clean cutlery, and put the pack back in the fridge right after breakfast or lunch.
  • Write the opening date on the pack, because the 3–5 days start then.

Can you freeze ham and cold cuts?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months in the freezer for ham and most cold cuts. Freeze them in small portions, with baking paper between the slices, so you can take out exactly what you need. Defrost them in the fridge and eat them within a day or two. The texture can become slightly wetter, which makes defrosted ham ideal for toasted sandwiches, quiches or pasta.

How to tell if ham has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: a slightly sour, unusual smell, a grey discolouration, or a sticky, slimy surface. Do not eat cold cuts that look strange or smell off. But remember: listeria cannot be seen or smelled, so if the use-by date has passed, the pack goes in the bin, however it smells.

Tips to waste less cold cuts

  • Buy small packs if you do not eat cold cuts every day.
  • Freeze part of a large pack straight away.
  • Log the opening date in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder before the 3–5 days are up.

Frequently asked questions

How long does sliced ham last once opened?

3 to 5 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, and never past the use-by date.

How long can pregnant people keep opened cold cuts?

2 to 3 days at most, because of listeria. The same applies to older people, very young children and people with a weakened immune system.

Can you eat ham past the use-by date?

No. Bacteria like listeria cannot be seen or smelled.

Can you freeze sliced ham?

Yes, for about 3 months. Freeze small portions with baking paper between the slices.

Is slimy ham safe?

No. A slimy or sticky surface, a sour smell or grey colour means it has gone off.
