STORAGE GUIDE

How long does mince (ground beef) last in the fridge?

Short answer: Once opened, raw mince keeps 1 day in the fridge according to the Voedingscentrum, and never past its use-by date. Cooked mince, such as bolognese, keeps 2 days. In the freezer, raw or cooked mince keeps about 3 months. Defrost it in the fridge and use it within 24 hours.

Why mince goes off faster than a steak

Bacteria such as E. coli and salmonella sit on the surface of meat. On a steak, searing the outside kills them. When meat is minced, those surface bacteria get spread all the way through. The Food Standards Agency explains that this is why mince and burgers must be cooked right through, and it is also why mince spoils faster than a whole piece of meat.

How to store raw mince

  • Bottom shelf, covered. Keep raw meat in its packaging or a sealed container on the bottom shelf, so juices cannot drip onto other food.
  • Cold enough. The FSA advises a fridge between 0 and 5°C; the Voedingscentrum advises 4°C.
  • Respect the use-by date. Mince has a use-by date because harmful bacteria can grow. Do not eat it after that date, even if it looks and smells fine.
  • Use it quickly. The Voedingscentrum gives 1 day in the fridge for raw mince.

Can you freeze mince?

Yes, and it is the easiest way to avoid throwing mince away. The FSA says you can freeze food right up until midnight on the use-by date. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months for raw mince in the freezer; the FSA says meat is usually best eaten within 2–3 months. After that it is not unsafe, but it can taste and smell rancid.

  • Freeze in small portions, so you only defrost what you need.
  • Wrap it well or use a freezer bag against freezer burn.
  • Write the date on it, and keep your freezer at −18°C.

How to defrost mince safely

Defrost mince in the fridge: take it out of the freezer a day before you need it, and put it on a covered plate or in a bowl on the bottom shelf. The FSA advises using food within 24 hours once it is fully defrosted, because from then on it goes off just like fresh mince. Short on time? Use the defrost setting of the microwave and cook the mince straight away.

Defrosted raw mince should not go back into the freezer as it is. Cook it first: you can freeze the cooked dish, but then only reheat it once.

Cooking mince: always all the way through

Unlike a whole steak, mince and burgers should never be eaten pink. The FSA advises checking that mince is steaming hot all the way through, that none of the meat is pink when you cut into it, and that the juices run clear. With a food thermometer, the middle should reach 70°C for 2 minutes.

Cooked mince and leftovers

Bolognese, chilli or meatballs: cool them and put them in the fridge within 1–2 hours. Cooked mince keeps 2 days in the fridge (the FSA says eat leftovers within 48 hours) or about 3 months in the freezer. Reheat leftovers only once, until steaming hot.

How to tell if mince has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: an unusual smell, a grey discolouration or a sticky surface. If you see any of these, throw it away. But do not rely on a sniff test for food with a use-by date: the FSA points out that you cannot smell the bacteria that cause food poisoning. Past the use-by date means in the bin, however it smells.

Tips to waste less mince

  • Freeze mince on the day you buy it if you will not cook it within a day.
  • Cook a big batch of sauce and freeze it in portions.
  • Log the use-by date in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder the day before.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat mince one day after the use-by date?

No. A use-by date is about safety. Do not eat mince after it, even if it looks and smells normal.

How long does cooked mince last in the fridge?

2 days. The Voedingscentrum gives 2 days for cooked mince, and the FSA advises eating leftovers within 48 hours.

Can you refreeze defrosted mince?

Not raw. Cook it first; you can then freeze the cooked dish, but reheat it only once.

Can mince be pink in the middle?

No. Mince and burgers must be cooked until steaming hot all the way through, with no pink meat and clear juices.

Is grey mince safe to eat?

The Voedingscentrum lists grey discolouration, an unusual smell and stickiness as signs of spoilage. When in doubt, throw it out, and never eat it past the use-by date.

How long can raw mince stay out of the fridge?

As short as possible. Never leave food that spoils quickly, like meat, out of the fridge for more than 2 hours.
