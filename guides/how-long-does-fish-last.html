STORAGE GUIDE

How long does fresh fish last in the fridge?

Short answer: Fresh fish keeps 1 to 2 days in the fridge: the Voedingscentrum gives 2 days for raw salmon and 1 day for raw cod, and advises eating fresh fish preferably on the day you buy it. Never eat it past its use-by date. In the freezer, raw salmon keeps about 3 months and cod about 6 months. Cooked fish keeps 2 days.

Fresh fish: best on the day you buy it

Fish spoils faster than most meat. The Voedingscentrum advises keeping fresh fish as close to freezing point as possible and eating it preferably on the same day, or the next day at the latest. Its storage guide gives 2 days in the fridge for raw salmon and 1 day for raw cod. Fresh fish has a use-by date: do not eat it after that date, because of the risk of harmful bacteria.

How to store fresh fish

  • In the coldest part of the fridge, usually the bottom shelf. The FSA advises a fridge between 0 and 5°C; the Voedingscentrum 4°C.
  • Wrapped and on ice. The Voedingscentrum's tip: wrap the fish in (reusable) cling film and cover it with plenty of ice cubes.
  • Keep it sealed, so the smell does not spread and juices do not drip onto other food.

Smoked salmon

Once opened, smoked salmon keeps 2 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. Keep it in the coldest spot, in a sealed container or its original packaging, and never eat it past the use-by date.

Can you freeze fish?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months for raw salmon and about 6 months for raw cod in the freezer. Freeze fish on the day you buy it, well wrapped, and write the date on it. You can freeze food right up until midnight on its use-by date, according to the FSA.

Defrost fish in the fridge, ideally starting the day before. In a hurry? The Voedingscentrum advises the microwave on a low setting for delicate products like fish (about 100 watts), turning it once or twice. Once defrosted, use it within 24 hours.

Cook fish thoroughly

The Voedingscentrum advises heating fish, shellfish and crustaceans well, because especially shellfish and crustaceans can carry pathogens. Fish is cooked when the flesh is opaque and flakes easily.

Cooked fish and leftovers

Cooked fish keeps 2 days in the fridge and about 3 months in the freezer, according to the Voedingscentrum's figures for cooked cod. Cool it quickly and refrigerate it within 2 hours.

How to tell if fish has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: an unusual smell and taste, and for white fish like cod a dull colour. Do not eat fish that looks strange or smells off. Fresh fish smells mild, of the sea; a strong, sour or ammonia-like smell means it goes in the bin. And past the use-by date, the date decides.

Tips to waste less fish

  • Buy fish on the day you cook it, or freeze it straight away.
  • Keep a few frozen fillets in the freezer for quick dinners.
  • Log fish in FreshGarant with its use-by date, so it gets cooked in time.
