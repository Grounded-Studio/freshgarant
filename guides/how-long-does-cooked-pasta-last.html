STORAGE GUIDE

How long does cooked pasta last in the fridge?

Short answer: Cooked pasta keeps 2 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, and about 3 months in the freezer. Cool it quickly and get it into the fridge within 2 hours. Dry, uncooked pasta keeps for longer than a year, and often up to a year past its best-before date.

How long is cooked pasta good?

The Voedingscentrum gives 2 days in the fridge for cooked pasta, the same as for other leftovers. The FSA's general rule for leftovers matches: eat them within 48 hours, or freeze them if you think you will not manage that. Pasta with a sauce follows the shortest time of its ingredients, so pasta with fish or chicken should not be kept longer than the pasta itself.

How to store cooked pasta

  • Cool it fast. The Voedingscentrum's rule for leftovers: let them cool quickly and never leave them out of the fridge for more than 2 hours. The FSA also says to get cooked food into the fridge within one to two hours.
  • Small portions cool faster. Spread the pasta out in shallow containers. Placing the container in a layer of cold water speeds this up.
  • Closed container. A drizzle of oil stops plain pasta from sticking together.
  • Write the date on it, so you know when the 2 days are up.

Can you freeze cooked pasta?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months in the freezer. Pasta in a sauce, like lasagne or a pasta bake, freezes best. Plain pasta can turn a bit soft; cook it slightly al dente if you plan to freeze it. Freeze it in portions, defrost in the fridge and reheat until steaming hot.

Reheating pasta

Leftover pasta can be eaten cold, for example as a pasta salad, if it was cooled and refrigerated properly. If you reheat it, the FSA advises heating it until steaming hot all the way through, and only once. Stir halfway in the microwave, because food can be hot at the edges and still cold in the middle. Dishes like lasagne heat more evenly in smaller portions.

Dry pasta

Uncooked dry pasta hardly spoils. The Voedingscentrum gives more than a year in a cool, dry, dark place, and says it is often fine up to a year past the best-before date, and possibly longer, as long as it looks normal and the pack is intact. Keep opened packs well closed to keep pantry insects out. Signs it has gone off: a musty or rancid smell, or insects.

Tips to waste less pasta

  • Weigh dry pasta before cooking: the Voedingscentrum recommends kitchen scales or a measuring cup for the right portions.
  • Turn leftovers into a pasta salad, frittata or pasta bake the next day.
  • Log leftovers in FreshGarant with a 2-day date, so they get eaten in time.

Frequently asked questions

How long does cooked pasta last in the fridge?

2 days, according to the Voedingscentrum; the FSA's rule for leftovers is 48 hours.

Can you freeze cooked pasta?

Yes, for about 3 months. Pasta in a sauce freezes best.

Can you eat cold pasta?

Yes, if it was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge, within 2 days.

How often can you reheat pasta?

Once, until steaming hot all the way through.

Does dry pasta expire?

It hardly spoils. It often keeps up to a year past the best-before date if the pack is intact and it smells normal.
