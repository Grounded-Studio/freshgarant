STORAGE GUIDE

How long does chicken last in the fridge?

Short answer: Raw chicken keeps 2 days in the fridge once the pack is opened, according to the Voedingscentrum, and never past its use-by date. Cooked chicken also keeps 2 days. In the freezer, raw or cooked chicken keeps about 3 months. Never wash raw chicken, and always cook it all the way through.

Why chicken needs extra care

Raw chicken often carries bacteria such as campylobacter, which the Food Standards Agency calls the main cause of bacterial food poisoning in the UK. A very small number of these bacteria can already make you ill. That is why chicken has a use-by date, and why storing, handling and cooking it correctly matters more than for most other food.

How to store raw chicken

  • Bottom shelf, covered. Keep raw chicken in its packaging or a sealed container on the bottom shelf, so juices cannot drip onto other food.
  • Cold enough. The FSA advises a fridge between 0 and 5°C; the Voedingscentrum advises 4°C.
  • Use-by is a hard limit. Do not eat chicken after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells normal.
  • Once opened: the Voedingscentrum gives 2 days in the fridge.

Never wash raw chicken

Rinsing chicken under the tap does not make it safer. The FSA warns that washing raw chicken splashes campylobacter onto your hands, worktop, clothes and utensils. Cooking kills the bacteria; washing only spreads them. Use a separate chopping board for raw meat, and wash your hands after touching it.

Can you freeze chicken?

Yes. You can freeze chicken right up until midnight on its use-by date, according to the FSA. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months in the freezer at −18°C; the FSA says meat is usually best within 2–3 months. Freeze it in portions and write the date on the bag.

Defrost chicken in the fridge, on a covered plate or in a bowl on the bottom shelf, starting a day before you need it. Once fully defrosted, use it within 24 hours. Small pieces can be defrosted in the microwave, but then cook them straight away.

Cook chicken all the way through

Poultry can carry bacteria all the way through the meat, so it must never be eaten pink. The FSA advises checking that the chicken is steaming hot all the way through, that none of the meat is pink when you cut into the thickest part, and that the juices run clear. With a thermometer, the middle should reach 70°C for 2 minutes.

Cooked chicken and leftovers

Let cooked chicken cool and put it in the fridge within 1–2 hours, covered. It keeps 2 days in the fridge, or about 3 months in the freezer. Leftovers can be eaten cold, or reheated once until steaming hot.

How to tell if chicken has gone off

The Voedingscentrum lists these signs: an unusual smell, a grey discolouration, a dried-out or sticky surface. If you notice any of them, throw it away. But a normal smell is no guarantee: the bacteria that cause food poisoning cannot be smelled, so never use a sniff test to decide on chicken past its use-by date.

Tips to waste less chicken

  • Freeze chicken on the day you buy it if you will not cook it within a day or two.
  • Cook extra and use the leftovers the next day in a wrap, salad or soup.
  • Log the use-by date in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder before it has to go.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat chicken one day after the use-by date?

No. The use-by date on chicken is about safety. Do not eat it afterwards, even if it looks and smells fine.

How long does cooked chicken last in the fridge?

2 days, according to the Voedingscentrum; the FSA advises eating leftovers within 48 hours.

Should you wash raw chicken?

No. Washing spreads campylobacter around your kitchen. Thorough cooking is what kills the bacteria.

Can you refreeze chicken?

Not raw after defrosting. Cook it first; you can freeze the cooked dish and reheat it once.

How do I know chicken is cooked?

It is steaming hot all the way through, no meat is pink in the thickest part and the juices run clear.
