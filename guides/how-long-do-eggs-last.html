STORAGE GUIDE

How long do eggs last in the fridge?

Short answer: Raw eggs keep about 4 weeks in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. A hard-boiled egg keeps up to a week in its shell, a peeled one 2 days. After the best-before date, eggs are still safe for a couple of days if you cook them thoroughly. You can freeze eggs out of their shell for about 3 months.

Where to keep eggs

The Voedingscentrum advises keeping eggs in their box in the fridge: that stops them drying out. The FSA says eggs should be stored in a cool, dry place, ideally the fridge, away from big temperature changes. Those changes cause condensation on the shell, which lets salmonella get from the outside into the egg more easily.

  • In the box. Eggs easily take on smells, so keep them away from strong-smelling food.
  • Point down. Eggs have an air pocket at the blunt end; storing them point down keeps it intact, so they stay good longer.

Eggs after the best-before date

Eggs have a best-before date, not a use-by date. The Voedingscentrum advises trying to eat them before that date: they are fresher and there is less chance of bacteria growing. The FSA says eggs are safe for a couple of days after the best-before date if they are cooked well, with both white and yolk fully set. So use older eggs for a hard-boiled egg, an omelette or baking, not for a soft-boiled egg or homemade mayonnaise.

Does the float test work?

Not for safety. The FSA explains that an egg floats because more air gets inside as it ages. The float test tells you roughly how old an egg is, not whether bacteria have grown inside it. Go by the date and how you store eggs instead.

How long do boiled eggs last?

According to the Voedingscentrum, a hard-boiled egg keeps up to a week in the fridge as long as it is still in its shell. Peeled, it keeps 2 days in a closed container. Keep a soft-boiled egg no longer than 2 days, because the yolk is not fully set and bacteria can grow. Cooling boiled eggs in cold water helps them dry out less.

Can you freeze eggs?

Yes, but not in their shell. The FSA advises cracking the eggs into a container, sealing it and freezing it. You can separate yolks and whites first, or beat the eggs so they are ready for scrambled eggs or an omelette. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months for beaten eggs in the freezer. Label the container. Hard-boiled eggs are less suitable for freezing.

How to tell if an egg has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: the egg has dried out or has an unusual smell. Crack doubtful eggs into a separate bowl first, so one bad egg does not ruin the rest of your dish.

Tips to waste less eggs

  • Use the oldest eggs first, and save them for dishes that are cooked thoroughly.
  • Beat leftover eggs and freeze them in portions.
  • Log the best-before date in FreshGarant, so you get a reminder in time.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat eggs after the best-before date?

For a couple of days, if you cook them thoroughly so white and yolk are fully set (FSA). Avoid soft-boiled or raw use.

Should eggs be kept in the fridge?

Yes, ideally. Keep them in their box, away from strong smells and big temperature changes.

Does the egg float test show if an egg is safe?

No. It shows roughly how old an egg is, not whether bacteria have grown inside.

How long do hard-boiled eggs last?

Up to a week in the fridge in their shell, 2 days once peeled.

Can you freeze eggs?

Yes, out of the shell: crack or beat them into a sealed container. They keep about 3 months.
