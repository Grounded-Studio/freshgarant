STORAGE GUIDE

How long does cheese last in the fridge?

Short answer: A piece of opened hard cheese, such as Gouda, keeps about 1 week in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. Sliced cheese keeps 4 days and grated cheese 2 days. Hard cheese can be frozen for 2 to 4 months. Mould on hard cheese can be cut away generously; on soft or grated cheese it cannot.

Hard cheese keeps well

Hard cheeses such as Gouda, Edam or mature cheddar contain little moisture, so they spoil slowly. The Voedingscentrum's storage guide gives about 1 week in the fridge for an opened piece of young-matured Gouda, and says unopened hard cheese can often be eaten up to 2 months after its best-before date, and possibly longer, as long as it still looks fine and the packaging is intact.

Slices and grated cheese have a much bigger surface and dry out or spoil faster: 4 days for slices and 2 days for grated cheese once opened.

How to store cheese

  • In the fridge, in a closed wrap. The Voedingscentrum recommends cheese paper or (reusable) cling film, which stops the cheese from sweating.
  • Keep it covered. Exposed cheese dries out and picks up smells from other food.
  • Close sliced and grated packs properly after each use.

What to do with mould on cheese

It depends on the cheese. For hard cheese such as Gouda or Edam, the Voedingscentrum says you can cut away mould generously, at least 1 centimetre around it, and eat the rest. That only applies to hard cheese. For soft cheese, cream cheese, sliced or grated cheese, mould can spread further than you can see: throw it away. The FSA adds that removing mould is never a full guarantee that unseen toxins are gone, which matters most for children, pregnant people, older people and anyone with a weakened immune system.

Can you freeze cheese?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum gives 2 to 4 months in the freezer for hard cheese. Freezing changes the texture: the cheese turns more crumbly, so frozen cheese is best for cooking, sauces, gratins or toasties. Grating cheese before you freeze it makes it easy to use straight from the freezer.

How to tell if cheese has gone off

For hard cheese, the Voedingscentrum names these signs: the cheese has dried out, has mould, or smells strange. A dried-out edge can be cut off; mould on hard cheese can be cut away generously, as above. Cheese that smells clearly off, or soft cheese with mould, goes in the bin.

Tips to waste less cheese

  • Buy a piece instead of slices if you do not eat cheese every day: it keeps longer.
  • Grate leftover ends and freeze them for pasta, soup or a gratin.
  • Log opened packs in FreshGarant, so sliced and grated cheese do not get forgotten.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat cheese after the best-before date?

Hard cheese often yes: up to 2 months and possibly longer if it still looks fine and the pack is intact. Look, smell and taste.

Can you cut mould off cheese?

Only off hard cheese such as Gouda or Edam: cut at least 1 cm around it. Soft, sliced or grated cheese with mould goes in the bin.

How long do cheese slices last once opened?

4 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. Grated cheese keeps 2 days.

Can you freeze cheese?

Yes, hard cheese for 2 to 4 months. It gets more crumbly, so use it for cooking.

Why does cheese sweat in the fridge?

In airtight plastic, moisture gets trapped. Cheese paper or reusable wrap helps prevent this.
