How long do avocados last?

Short answer: Avocados keep 1 to 6 days in a fruit bowl, depending on how ripe they are, according to the Voedingscentrum. Let them ripen at room temperature, then move them to the fridge, where they keep longer. Keep half an avocado with its stone in, covered, in the fridge. Mashed or in pieces, avocado can be frozen for 8 to 12 months.

Ripen first, then the fridge

How long an avocado keeps depends on how ripe it is when you buy it. Avocados keep ripening after picking, the Voedingscentrum explains: you can let them ripen in the fruit bowl first and then keep them in the fridge, where they last longer. In the fruit bowl, the storage guide gives 1 to 6 days.

  • Hard avocado: leave it in the fruit bowl until it gives slightly when you press gently.
  • Need it faster? Avocados ripen faster next to apples, bananas and pears, in the fruit bowl or in a closed bag, according to the Voedingscentrum.
  • Ripe avocado: into the fridge, to slow it down.

How to keep half an avocado

The Voedingscentrum's tip: leave the stone in the half you are keeping, cover it with (reusable) cling film and put it in the fridge. That limits browning of the flesh. Eat it within a day or so.

Brown flesh: spoiled or not?

Brown discolouration of the flesh does not necessarily mean the avocado has gone off, says the Voedingscentrum. A soft or bruised spot can be cut away if the rest of the avocado is still good. But throw it away if it is mouldy, slimy, rotten or smells off. The FSA also advises not eating food that clearly contains mould.

Can you freeze avocado?

Yes. The Voedingscentrum says you can mash avocado or cut it into pieces and freeze it, for 8 to 12 months. The texture does change, so after freezing it is best for guacamole or smoothies rather than slices on toast. A squeeze of lemon or lime juice before freezing helps keep the colour.

How to tell if an avocado has gone off

Signs from the Voedingscentrum: mould, slimy or rotten flesh, or an unusual smell. Dark stringy fibres or a few brown spots alone are not a reason to throw the whole avocado away: cut them out and use the rest.

Tips to waste fewer avocados

  • Buy avocados at different stages of ripeness, so you always have one ready.
  • Put ripe avocados in the fridge straight away.
  • Mash and freeze avocados that are about to go over, for guacamole later.
  • Log avocados in FreshGarant, so you catch them at the right moment.

Frequently asked questions

How long do avocados last?

1 to 6 days in the fruit bowl, depending on ripeness. Ripe avocados keep longer in the fridge.

Should you keep avocados in the fridge?

Once they are ripe, yes. Let hard avocados ripen at room temperature first.

How do you keep half an avocado?

Leave the stone in, cover it with cling film and put it in the fridge.

Is a brown avocado still good?

Brown flesh is not necessarily spoiled. Cut away brown or bruised spots; throw it away if it is mouldy, slimy or smells off.

Can you freeze avocado?

Yes, mashed or in pieces, for 8 to 12 months. Use it for guacamole or smoothies.

How do you know when an avocado is ripe?

A ripe avocado gives slightly when you press it gently. A hard one needs a few more days in the fruit bowl; a very soft one should be eaten or frozen straight away.
