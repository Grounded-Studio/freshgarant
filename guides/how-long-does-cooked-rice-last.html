STORAGE GUIDE

How long does cooked rice last in the fridge?

Short answer: Eat cooked rice within 24 hours, as the UK Food Standards Agency advises; the Dutch Voedingscentrum gives a maximum of 2 days. Cool it and put it in the fridge within an hour, reheat it only once, and make sure it is steaming hot. In the freezer, cooked rice keeps about 3 months.

Why cooked rice needs extra care

Uncooked rice can contain spores of a bacterium called Bacillus cereus. According to the Food Standards Agency, these spores can survive cooking. The longer cooked rice stays at room temperature, the more likely it is that the bacteria or their toxins make the rice unsafe to eat.

That is also why reheating does not fix rice that has been left out. As the FSA puts it, it is not the reheating that causes the problem, but the way the rice was stored before it was reheated.

How to store cooked rice safely

  1. Cool it fast. Chill leftover rice as quickly as possible, ideally within one hour. Do not leave it to cool in the rice cooker, steamer or pan.
  2. Use small portions. Dividing rice into smaller, shallow containers helps it cool faster. Placing the container in a little cold water speeds this up even more.
  3. Cover it and put it in the fridge. The FSA advises a fridge between 0 and 5°C; the Voedingscentrum advises setting it to 4°C.
  4. Write the date on it. Then you know exactly when it has to be eaten.

How long is cooked rice good in the fridge?

The UK Food Standards Agency says to keep rice in the fridge for no more than one day before you eat or reheat it. The Dutch Voedingscentrum lists a maximum of 2 days for cooked rice. If you want the safest option, eat it the next day at the latest.

Cold rice is fine to eat, for example in a rice salad, as long as it was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge.

Can you freeze cooked rice?

Yes. Freezing is the best way to keep rice longer than a day. The FSA advises getting rice into the freezer within an hour of cooking, so bacteria cannot multiply and produce toxins. Freeze it in portions so it cools quickly and you only defrost what you need.

The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months for cooked rice in the freezer, which should be set to −18°C. Defrost frozen rice in the fridge, then reheat it until it is steaming hot and eat it straight away.

How to reheat rice

  • Only once. Never reheat rice more than once.
  • Steaming hot all the way through. Stir halfway in the microwave: food can be hot at the edges and still cold in the middle.
  • Takeaway rice needs extra care. Restaurants sometimes cook rice in advance and reheat it before serving, so eat takeaway rice soon after it arrives and do not reheat it again.

How to tell if rice has gone off

Rice that smells sour, feels slimy or shows mould has clearly spoiled, so throw it away. But the reverse is not true: the bacteria and toxins that cause food poisoning cannot be seen or smelled. Rice that looks and smells fine can still be unsafe if it was left out too long or kept too long. Go by time, not by smell.

Tips to waste less rice

  • Cook the right amount. The Voedingscentrum recommends a measuring cup or kitchen scales to get portions right.
  • Freeze the leftovers straight away instead of putting them in the fridge "for later".
  • Plan a rice dish for the next day, such as fried rice or a rice salad, so the leftovers get used within the safe window.
  • Log it in FreshGarant with a 1-day date, so you get a reminder before it has to go.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat cooked rice the next day?

Yes, if it was cooled quickly (ideally within an hour) and kept in the fridge. The FSA advises eating it within 24 hours; the Voedingscentrum gives a maximum of 2 days.

Can you reheat rice twice?

No. The FSA advises never reheating rice more than once, and making sure it is steaming hot all the way through when you do.

Is 3-day-old rice safe to eat?

No. Three days is longer than both the FSA advice (one day) and the Voedingscentrum maximum (2 days), so throw it away.

How long can cooked rice sit out?

As short as possible. The FSA advises getting it into the fridge within an hour; the Voedingscentrum says never leave leftovers out of the fridge for more than 2 hours.

Can you freeze cooked rice?

Yes. Freeze it within an hour of cooking, in portions. It keeps about 3 months. Defrost it in the fridge and reheat it until steaming hot.

Can you eat rice cold?

Yes, as long as it was cooled quickly and kept in the fridge, and you eat it within the same time limits.
