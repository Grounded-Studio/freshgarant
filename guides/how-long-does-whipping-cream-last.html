STORAGE GUIDE

How long does whipping cream last after opening?

Short answer: Opened whipping cream keeps 4 to 6 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, and whipped cream as well. An unopened carton follows the date on the pack. Whipped cream can be frozen for about 1 month; liquid cream is less suitable for freezing.

How long is opened cream good?

The Voedingscentrum gives 4 to 6 days in the fridge for opened whipping cream, and the same for cream you have already whipped. If the carton says "once opened, use within x days", follow that instead. Long-life cream can be kept in the cupboard until opened; after that it belongs in the fridge.

Past the date?

Cream with a best-before date does not have to be thrown away the moment the date passes. The Voedingscentrum advises looking, smelling and tasting a little to judge the quality. If your cream carries a use-by date instead, the date decides: the FSA warns that you cannot smell the bacteria that cause food poisoning.

How to store cream

  • Back in the fridge straight away after use, at 4°C. That keeps it good for longer.
  • Use a clean spoon when you scoop from a tub, to avoid contamination with yeasts and moulds.
  • Close the carton or cover the bowl, so the cream does not take on smells from the fridge.

Can you freeze cream?

Whipped cream freezes well: the Voedingscentrum gives about 1 month. A practical trick is to pipe or spoon small dollops onto a tray, freeze them and then put them in a bag; they are perfect on hot chocolate or a dessert. Liquid cream is less suitable for freezing because the structure changes. Defrosted liquid cream is still fine in sauces, soups or baking, but it will not whip well any more.

How to tell if cream has gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: lumps, cream that turns watery, or a sour smell. Any of these means the cream goes in the bin. Mould on the surface also means throwing the whole carton away.

Cream out of the fridge

Cream is a perishable dairy product. The Voedingscentrum advises never leaving food that spoils quickly out of the fridge for more than 2 hours. That also goes for a dessert or cake with whipped cream on the table: put it back in the fridge between servings, and throw away whipped cream that has stood in a warm room for an afternoon.

Long-life cream

Long-life (UHT) cream can be kept in the cupboard until it is opened, until the date on the pack. Once opened, it is treated like fresh cream: in the fridge, and used within the days stated on the pack.

Tips to waste less cream

  • Bought too much for a dessert? Whip the rest and freeze it in dollops.
  • Use leftover cream in a pasta sauce, soup, scrambled eggs or a curry.
  • Log the opening date in FreshGarant, so you know when the 4 to 6 days are up.

Frequently asked questions

How long does opened whipping cream last?

4 to 6 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, unless the carton says otherwise.

How long does whipped cream last in the fridge?

Also 4 to 6 days, covered, at 4°C.

Can you freeze whipped cream?

Yes, for about 1 month. Freeze small dollops on a tray, then put them in a bag.

Can you freeze liquid cream?

It is less suitable: the structure changes and it will not whip well. Defrosted, it is still fine for sauces and soups.

Can you use cream after the best-before date?

Often yes. Look, smell and taste a little first. Lumps, a watery texture or a sour smell mean it has gone off.

How long can whipped cream stay out of the fridge?

As short as possible, and never more than 2 hours. Put desserts with cream back in the fridge between servings.
