STORAGE GUIDE

How long do potatoes last?

Short answer: Raw potatoes keep about 1 month in a cool, dark, dry place, according to the Voedingscentrum. Cooked potatoes keep 2 days in the fridge and about 3 months in the freezer. Remove sprouts and cut away green parts generously before cooking. Raw potatoes are not suitable for freezing.

Where to store raw potatoes

The Voedingscentrum advises storing potatoes in a dry, dark and cool place, where they keep about a month. In a warm spot they start to sprout; light turns them green. A cellar, a cool cupboard or a paper bag in a dark corner works well.

Potatoes in the fridge? The sources differ

This is a point where the two food safety bodies give different advice:

  • The UK Food Standards Agency says you can store potatoes in the fridge or in a cool, dark, dry place. It refers to a study, reviewed by an expert committee, which found that keeping potatoes in a home fridge does not materially increase the sugars that form acrylamide when frying or roasting.
  • The Dutch Voedingscentrum says you can keep potatoes in the fridge if you have no cool, dark place, but only potatoes for boiling, not for frying or roasting, because more sugars form in the fridge that can turn into acrylamide.

The cautious choice: a cool, dark cupboard first; the fridge if you have nothing better, ideally for potatoes you plan to boil.

Sprouts and green spots

Sprouted potatoes can still be eaten: remove the sprouts and cut off the spot. Green and damaged parts are different. The FSA explains that green, damaged and sprouted parts can contain high levels of natural toxins called glycoalkaloids, which can cause stomach pain, vomiting and diarrhoea. Poisoning is extremely rare, but cut green parts away generously. Storing potatoes cool, dark and dry prevents this.

Cooked potatoes

Leftover boiled potatoes keep 2 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. Let them cool quickly, put them in a closed container and never leave them out of the fridge for more than 2 hours.

Can you freeze potatoes?

Not raw: the Voedingscentrum calls raw potatoes unsuitable for freezing. Cooked potatoes do freeze well, for about 3 months. The FSA adds that you can freeze cooked potatoes or potatoes parboiled for 5 minutes, and roast them straight from the freezer with a little oil, until steaming hot all the way through.

How to tell if potatoes have gone off

The Voedingscentrum names these signs: potatoes that are mouldy, dried out or rotten. Soft, wrinkly potatoes that are otherwise fine can still be used, for example for mash or soup.

Tips to waste less potatoes

  • Buy a smaller bag if you do not eat potatoes several times a week.
  • Cook extra and use the leftovers for fried potatoes, a potato salad or a frittata.
  • Parboil and freeze potatoes that are starting to sprout, ready for roasting.
