How long does yoghurt last after opening?

Short answer: Opened yoghurt keeps 5 to 7 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. An unopened pot follows the date on the pack. Yoghurt with a best-before date is often still fine afterwards: look, smell and taste. Freezing is possible, but the texture changes.

Best before or use by?

Like milk, yoghurt can carry either a best-before or a use-by date; the FSA notes that the manufacturer decides which one. A best-before date is about quality, so yoghurt is often fine for a while after it. The Voedingscentrum advises not to throw yoghurt away straight after that date, but to look, smell and taste a little first. A use-by date is about safety: then the date decides, because a sniff test cannot detect the bacteria that cause food poisoning.

How long does opened yoghurt last?

The Voedingscentrum gives 5 to 7 days in the fridge for opened yoghurt. If the pot says "once opened, eat within x days", follow that. Big tubs last as long as you keep them clean and cold, which brings us to the two most important habits.

How to store yoghurt

  • Always use a clean spoon. The Voedingscentrum points out that a clean spoon prevents contamination with yeasts and moulds. Never eat straight from the tub and put it back.
  • Straight back into the fridge. Put yoghurt back quickly after use, at 4°C, so it stays good for longer.
  • Close the lid. Or cover the pot, so it does not dry out or take on smells.

Can you freeze yoghurt?

You can, but the Voedingscentrum calls yoghurt less suitable for freezing because the structure changes: it can turn grainy or watery once defrosted. It is still fine to use in smoothies, sauces, curries or baking. Freeze small portions, for example in an ice cube tray, so you can take out exactly what you need.

How to tell if yoghurt has gone off

The Voedingscentrum lists these signs: lumps, yoghurt that turns watery, smells sour or tastes bitter. Note that yoghurt is naturally a little sour, so it is about a sour smell that is clearly off. A thin layer of liquid on top (whey) on its own is normal: stir it back in. Any mould means the whole pot goes in the bin. The FSA advises not eating food with visible mould, because removing the mouldy part does not guarantee that unseen toxins are gone.

Tips to waste less yoghurt

  • Finish an open tub first before opening a new one.
  • Use yoghurt that needs to go in a marinade, dressing, curry, cake or smoothie.
  • Log the opening date in FreshGarant, so you know when the 5–7 days are up.

Frequently asked questions

Can you eat yoghurt after the best-before date?

Often yes. Look, smell and taste a little first. If it is not lumpy, bitter or clearly sour, it is fine.

How long does opened yoghurt last?

5 to 7 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum, unless the pot says otherwise.

Is the liquid on top of yoghurt bad?

No, that is whey and it is normal. Stir it back in. Watery yoghurt combined with lumps or an off smell is a different story.

Can you freeze yoghurt?

Yes, but the texture changes. Use defrosted yoghurt in smoothies, sauces or baking.

Can you scrape mould off yoghurt?

No. Throw the whole pot away. Removing the mouldy part does not guarantee that hidden toxins are gone.
