How long does lettuce last in the fridge?

Short answer: A whole head of lettuce keeps 2 to 5 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum. Bagged, pre-cut salad keeps 2 days once opened, and never past its use-by date. Lettuce cannot be frozen. Wilted lettuce is not automatically spoiled; slimy, mouldy or bad-smelling lettuce is.

Whole lettuce versus bagged salad

A whole head of lettuce keeps 2 to 5 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum's storage guide. Pre-cut, bagged salad is more delicate: 2 days once opened. Cutting damages the leaves, so bacteria can grow more easily. That is why bagged salad often has a use-by date, and the Voedingscentrum says not to eat pre-cut vegetables after that date because of the risk of harmful bacteria.

How to keep lettuce fresh for longer

  • Trim the stem and give it water. The Voedingscentrum suggests cutting a thin slice off the bottom and placing the head in a small bowl of water in the fridge.
  • Or use a damp tea towel. Wrapping the base of the lettuce in a damp tea towel also helps.
  • Keep it away from apples, bananas and pears. Lettuce stored next to them spoils faster.
  • Close opened bags, or move the leaves to a container with a piece of kitchen paper.

Should you wash lettuce?

The FSA advises washing fruit and vegetables with water before you eat them, to remove dirt and bacteria from the outside: under a running tap or in a bowl of fresh water. Bagged salad that says "washed and ready to eat" does not need washing again. Wash lettuce just before eating rather than before storing it, so it stays dry in the fridge.

Can you freeze lettuce?

No. The Voedingscentrum calls lettuce unsuitable for freezing: the leaves turn limp and watery once defrosted. Spinach and other cooking greens are a different story, but salad leaves are best eaten fresh.

Wilted or spoiled?

Wilted lettuce is not automatically spoiled, the Voedingscentrum points out. You can often revive limp leaves in a bowl of ice-cold water for a few minutes. But throw lettuce away if it is mouldy, slimy or smells off. For bagged salad past its use-by date, the date decides.

Dry lettuce lasts longer

Moisture on the leaves makes lettuce go slimy faster. If you have washed lettuce but will not eat all of it, dry it well, for example in a salad spinner or between sheets of kitchen paper, and store it in a container with a dry piece of kitchen paper. Replace the paper when it gets damp. Pre-dressed salad does not keep: dress only what you will eat now.

Tips to waste less lettuce

  • Buy a whole head instead of a bag if you will not finish it within two days.
  • Plan salads early in the week, and save sturdier vegetables for later.
  • Log bagged salad in FreshGarant with its use-by date, so it does not get forgotten at the back of the fridge.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a head of lettuce last?

2 to 5 days in the fridge, according to the Voedingscentrum.

How long does bagged salad last once opened?

2 days, and never past its use-by date.

Can you eat wilted lettuce?

Yes, wilted is not spoiled. A few minutes in ice-cold water often crisps it up again. Slimy, mouldy or smelly lettuce goes in the bin.

Can you freeze lettuce?

No, salad leaves turn limp and watery after freezing.

Why does my lettuce go off so fast?

Storing it next to apples, bananas or pears speeds up spoiling. Keep it apart, and keep the base moist.

Can you keep dressed salad?

Not really: dressing makes the leaves wilt within hours. Dress only the portion you will eat right away.
