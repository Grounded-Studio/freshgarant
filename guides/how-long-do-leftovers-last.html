STORAGE GUIDE

How long do leftovers last in the fridge?

Short answer: Leftovers keep 2 days in the fridge: the Voedingscentrum gives 2 days and the FSA says eat them within 48 hours. In the freezer they keep about 3 months. Cool leftovers quickly, get them into the fridge within 2 hours, and reheat them only once, until steaming hot. Rice is the exception: eat it within 24 hours.

The 2-day rule

For most cooked leftovers, both sources agree: the Voedingscentrum gives 2 days in the fridge, and the FSA says to eat leftovers within 48 hours or freeze them if you think you will not manage that. The fridge slows bacteria down, but does not stop them. If you will not eat it within two days, freeze it on day one, not on day two.

Step 1: cool quickly

The FSA calls the range between 8 and 63°C the danger zone: at those temperatures bacteria grow fastest. So leftovers should not stand on the stove or counter for hours.

  • Within 2 hours into the fridge. The Voedingscentrum says never leave leftovers out of the fridge for more than 2 hours; the FSA says within one to two hours.
  • Divide into small portions. They cool much faster than a full pan.
  • Use cold water. Place the pan or container in a layer of cold water.
  • Cover them and put the date on the container.

Step 2: store them cold

The FSA advises a fridge between 0 and 5°C, the Voedingscentrum 4°C. Keep cooked leftovers separate from raw meat, and preferably above it, so nothing can drip onto them.

Step 3: reheat once, until steaming hot

Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot all the way through, not just warm. The FSA advises reheating only once, because every temperature change gives bacteria another chance to grow. The Voedingscentrum also advises against reheating leftovers a second time. In the microwave, stir halfway: food can be hot at the edges and still cold in the middle. Leftovers can also be eaten cold, if they were cooked properly, cooled and refrigerated within two hours.

Freezing leftovers

A freezer works like a pause button, says the FSA: most bacteria cannot grow in it. The Voedingscentrum gives about 3 months for leftovers in the freezer at −18°C. Freeze them as soon as possible, in portions, and label them with name and date. Defrost in the fridge and use them within 24 hours of defrosting. Food you cooked from frozen ingredients can be frozen again once cooked, but reheat it only once after that.

Exceptions to keep in mind

  • Rice: the FSA advises eating it within 24 hours, because of Bacillus cereus.
  • Takeaway food: it may already have been reheated, so eat it soon and do not reheat it again.
  • Vulnerable people (pregnant people, young children, older people, people with a weakened immune system) should be extra strict with timing.

Tips to waste fewer leftovers

  • Cook the right amount, or plan the leftovers as tomorrow's lunch.
  • Freeze single portions straight away for busy evenings.
  • Log leftovers in FreshGarant with a 2-day date, so they do not get lost at the back of the fridge.

Frequently asked questions

How long do leftovers last in the fridge?

2 days: the Voedingscentrum says 2 days and the FSA 48 hours.

Can you eat leftovers after 3 days?

No. That is longer than both sources advise. Freeze leftovers on day one if you will not eat them in time.

Can you put hot food in the fridge?

Cool it first, but get it into the fridge within 1 to 2 hours. Dividing it into small portions helps.

How many times can you reheat leftovers?

Once, until steaming hot all the way through.

How long can you freeze leftovers?

About 3 months, according to the Voedingscentrum. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours.
