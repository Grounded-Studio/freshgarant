STORAGE GUIDE

How long do bananas last?

Short answer: Bananas keep 3 to 7 days in a fruit bowl, according to the Voedingscentrum. Keep them out of the fridge, because they do not cope well with cold, and away from other fruit. Peeled and frozen, bananas keep 8 to 12 months. Brown or black skin does not mean a banana has gone off.

Keep bananas out of the fridge

The Voedingscentrum's advice is clear: bananas do not cope well with cold, so store them outside the fridge. In a fruit bowl they keep 3 to 7 days, depending on how ripe they were when you bought them.

Bananas and other fruit

Bananas affect the fruit around them, and the other way round. According to the Voedingscentrum, a lot of other fruit ripens or spoils faster when stored next to bananas, and bananas ripen faster next to apples and pears. Use that to your advantage:

  • Want them to last? Keep bananas apart from other fruit.
  • Want them to ripen? Put green bananas next to apples or pears.
  • Wrap the stems. The Voedingscentrum says covering the top of a bunch with (reusable) cling film helps bananas keep longer.

Brown or black bananas

A black skin does not mean a banana has gone off, says the Voedingscentrum: check the flesh. If it smells normal, you can still use it. The FSA agrees that overripe fruit like brown bananas can be eaten normally, as long as there is no mould. Very ripe bananas are perfect for banana bread, pancakes, porridge or smoothies. Throw a banana away if it is mouldy or rotten.

Can you freeze bananas?

Yes, and frozen bananas keep 8 to 12 months, according to the Voedingscentrum. Remove the peel first and cut the banana into pieces if you like, then store it in a sealed container or freezer bag. Frozen banana pieces are great straight from the freezer in smoothies, or blended into instant "ice cream". Freeze bananas when they are ripe or overripe, so they are sweet.

How to tell if a banana has gone off

Look past the skin. Signs a banana is no longer good: mould, rotten or leaking flesh, or a fermented, alcohol-like smell. A soft, brown banana with flesh that smells normal is just very ripe.

What to do with overripe bananas

Bananas that are too soft to eat as they are still have plenty of uses. Mash them into banana bread, muffins or pancakes; blend them into a smoothie; stir them through porridge; or use them as a natural sweetener in baking. Peel and freeze any you cannot use straight away, so you always have bananas ready for a smoothie.

Tips to waste fewer bananas

  • Buy bananas at different stages of ripeness, so they do not all ripen at once.
  • Peel and freeze bananas the moment they get too ripe to eat.
  • Log bananas in FreshGarant, so you remember to bake or freeze them in time.

Frequently asked questions

How long do bananas last?

3 to 7 days in a fruit bowl, according to the Voedingscentrum.

Should you keep bananas in the fridge?

No. Bananas do not cope well with cold. Keep them in a fruit bowl, away from other fruit.

Are black bananas safe to eat?

Yes, if the flesh smells normal and there is no mould. They are perfect for baking or smoothies.

Can you freeze bananas?

Yes, peeled, for 8 to 12 months.

Why do my bananas ripen so fast?

Apples and pears nearby make bananas ripen faster. Keep them apart and wrap the stems.

Is a banana with brown spots inside safe?

Brown spots usually mean the banana is very ripe. If the flesh smells normal and there is no mould or rot, it is fine to eat or bake with.
