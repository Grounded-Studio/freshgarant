What to Cook Tonight With What's in Your Fridge

Published 5 November 2026 · 2 min read

It's six o'clock, the fridge is half full and nothing looks like a meal. Most of the food we waste dies right here: not because there was nothing to eat, but because nobody saw the dinner in it.

Start with what has to go first

Don't start from a recipe. Start from the product that expires soonest — the chicken with today's date, the half bag of spinach, the open tub of cream cheese. That one item decides tonight's dish; everything else fills in around it.

A simple 3-step method

  1. Pick one "must use" item. Whatever expires today or tomorrow.
  2. Add a base you always have. Pasta, rice, potatoes, bread, eggs or wraps. Nearly every pantry has one.
  3. Add one or two extras for flavour. An onion, garlic, a tin of tomatoes, cheese, a handful of herbs.

Chicken + pasta + spinach and garlic is dinner. So are eggs + bread + the last peppers, or rice + leftover vegetables + soy sauce.

Five dishes that take almost anything

DishGood for using up
Omelette or frittataVegetables, cheese, ham, cooked potatoes
Stir-fry with rice or noodlesAny vegetables, chicken, tofu, leftovers
PastaSpinach, tomatoes, cream, cheese, mushrooms
SoupSoft vegetables, half onions, stock, leftover rice
Wraps or toastiesCheese, chicken, avocado, salad leaves
