How Long Does Food Last in the Fridge? (Chart)

Published 26 October 2026 · 2 min read

The date on the pack is one thing — but how long is that chicken good once it's in your fridge, or those leftovers from last night? Here's a clear, conservative chart.

These times assume a fridge at 4 °C or colder. Always follow the packaging first: a use-by date or a "once opened, consume within…" instruction beats this chart.

Meat, fish and leftovers

ProductIn the fridge
Minced meat (raw)1 day
Fresh fish (raw)1 day
Chicken and other poultry (raw)1–2 days
Steak, pork chops (raw)2–3 days
Sliced deli meat, once opened2–3 days
Leftovers and home-cooked mealsmax. 2 days
Cooked rice1 day — cool it quickly
Homemade soup2 days

Dairy and eggs

ProductIn the fridge
Milk, once opened3–4 days
Yogurt, once opened3–5 days
Soft cheese (brie, mozzarella)about 1 week, or per pack
Hard cheese, wrapped3–4 weeks
Butteruntil the best before date
Eggsuntil the best before date

Fruit and vegetables

ProductIn the fridge
Cut fruit and vegetables2–3 days
Bagged salad, once opened1–2 days
Leafy greens (spinach, lettuce)3–5 days
Broccoli, courgette, peppersabout 1 week
Carrots2–3 weeks

Not everything belongs in the fridge: bananas, tomatoes, potatoes, onions and bread keep better outside it.

Five ways to make food last longer

  1. Keep your fridge at 4 °C. Many fridges run warmer than you think — a cheap thermometer tells you.
  2. Raw meat and fish on the bottom shelf, so juices can't drip onto other food.
  3. Cool leftovers quickly and put them in the fridge within two hours, in a closed container.
  4. First in, first out: put new groceries at the back and older ones at the front.
  5. Freeze in time — before the use-by date, not after.

These are general guidelines for food stored at the right temperature. Always follow the instructions on the packaging, and when in doubt, throw it out. Sources: Voedingscentrum (Netherlands Nutrition Centre) and the EU rules on date marking.
